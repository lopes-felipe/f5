- Following is now automatic: the conversation follows new content while it is scrolled to the
  end, pauses when you scroll up, and resumes when you return to the end. The Following switch and
  the "Follow new messages by default" setting are removed, and reopening a thread keeps the saved
  reading position instead of jumping to the end.
- The floating Jump to latest control sits centered beside the Working indicator within the
  conversation width, appears only once a conversation has messages, and shows its new-content dot
  to the right of the button.
